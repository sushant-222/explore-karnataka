Welcome to Karnataka

Karnataka is a state in southern India known for its historical monuments, beautiful landscapes, waterfalls, hill stations and rich cultural heritage.

Popular Places

Mysuru

Famous for Mysore Palace, culture and heritage.

Coorg

A beautiful hill region known for coffee plantations and scenic landscapes.

Hampi

A UNESCO World Heritage site famous for its ancient ruins and temples.

Historical Karnataka

Hampi

Hampi is one of Karnataka's most famous historical destinations. The ruins represent the architectural and cultural heritage of the Vijayanagara Empire.

The region contains temples, monuments, stone structures and beautiful landscapes.

Waterfalls & Hill Stations

Jog Falls

One of Karnataka's well-known waterfalls surrounded by the natural beauty of the Western Ghats.

Coorg

A popular hill destination surrounded by greenery, hills and coffee plantations.
